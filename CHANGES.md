# Changes

## Unreleased

- **No background sound.** The museum entrance and the cave no longer play a synthesised background sound: it did
  not sound like the places it was meant for.
- **Nobody gets stuck.** In the atrium house, the theatre and the cliffside meeting room, the floor you can walk on
  now keeps clear of walls, railings and furniture, so walking along a railing or into a corner never traps you.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-27) — ALPHA

Needs VR room (mod_vrroom) 0.2.0 or later.

- **Walking up and down.** Five rooms now carry a walk mesh, so visitors can walk up stairs and along floors at
  different heights, and are stopped by walls and edges: the museum entrance, the theatre stage, the atrium house,
  the cliffside meeting room and the cave. The museum entrance has a new staircase to its upper gallery.
- **The museum entrance** is no longer flat grey. It has stone walls, a terrazzo floor, marble columns and an oak
  gallery floor, and a street and forecourt round it with its name over a new entrance. Inside are English signs a
  language teacher can use (opening times, admission prices, a floor guide, exhibit labels, visitor rules), a clock,
  a swinging pendulum, a background sound and an opening view.
- **The cave** no longer floats in the sky. Visitors can walk out of its mouth to the foot of the hill outside, where
  a line of boulders and a sign mark the end of the path. It has lanterns along the path, moving bats and trees,
  show-cave signs in English, a background sound and an opening view.
- The museum entrance and the cave use VR room 0.2.0's room settings for an opening view, a background sound and
  reflections. The other 18 rooms are unchanged.
- **Licences and credits.** Every room keeps its source licence, and its credit is shown in VR room:
  - CC0 1.0: art gallery, mountain lake, library, mini market, school building, science laboratory, space
    station, town square.
  - CC BY 3.0: cave, classroom, forest camp, house, kitchen, theatre stage, museum entrance hall, workshop.
  - CC BY 4.0: cliffside meeting room, round table hall, spaceship corridor.
  - MPL 2.0: atrium house.
  - The museum entrance and the cave are adaptations, allowed by CC BY 3.0. Their credits now say what was added.
    The added parts were made by script for this pack.
  - Each walk mesh is derived from its room and carries the room's licence. README.md lists every source.
