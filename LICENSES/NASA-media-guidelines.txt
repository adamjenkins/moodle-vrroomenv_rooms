NASA 3D Resources: usage note
=============================

Rooms in this pack that come from NASA 3D Resources
(https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources) are used under the terms that collection states:
its models are "free and without copyright".

NASA's media usage guidelines apply:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

- Use of these models does not state or imply endorsement by NASA or by any NASA employee.
- NASA's insignia, logos and other identifiers are not used in this pack and must not be added.
- Where a model page names a contractor as its source, that source is named in the room's credit
  line in environments.json and in README.md.
